Our Story

Born from a jingling collar and a faded phone number.

In 2019, our rescue hound Bruno sprinted after a deer during a rainy mountain hike. When we found him an hour later, his stamped aluminum pet store tag was badly scratched, the phone number was illegible, and the thin split ring was nearly bent open.

That frightening afternoon sparked a realization: pet tags are the single most critical lifelines our companions wear, yet most are made from cheap stamped tin that fades, jingles endlessly, and fails when you need it most.

We founded PAWTAG to build the hardware we demanded for our own pets: aerospace-grade alloys, silent collar fit, and optical fiber-laser engraving that survives mud, river swims, and a lifetime of adventures.

  • ✓100% founded and run by lifelong pet parents and craftsmen
  • ✓Every single tag is individually laser-engraved and hand-inspected
  • ✓Zero hollow stampings, toxic alloys, or painted ink that rubs off

Our Personalization Process

How your custom tag comes to life.

Every PAWTAG passes through four meticulous artisan stages before being carefully packaged and dispatched from our studio.

01

Silhouette & Size Selection

Choose from classic round, modern hexagon, arched silhouette, or playful bone shapes, scaled proportionately from petite cats up to giant breeds.

02

Typographic Optimization

Our studio calibrates kerning and visual hierarchy for your companion's name, primary and backup emergency numbers, and microchip or medical alerts.

03

Fiber-Laser Deep Vaporization

Industrial optical lasers vaporize 0.35mm of metal depth, producing sharp, dark, permanent characters that can never be rubbed or scratched away.

04

Hand-Buffing & Stress Testing

Each piece is deburred by hand to eliminate fur-snagging burs, fitted with our reinforced split ring, and pull-tested before final velvet-pouch packaging.
